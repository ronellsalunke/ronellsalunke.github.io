# ModeLLM Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

ModeLLM is an Android app for browsing the [models.dev](https://models.dev) catalog of AI models and providers.
This policy explains what data the app handles.
The short version: ModeLLM collects nothing, tracks nothing, and stores everything on your device.

## Data the app collects

None.
ModeLLM does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to the developer or to any third party.

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics or crash reporting.
- No advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

## Network access

The app requests two Android permissions: `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`.
It uses them for one purpose: downloading the public models.dev catalog.

- Catalog data (`api.json`, `models.json`, `search-index.json`) is fetched from `https://models.dev`.
- Provider and lab logos are fetched from `https://models.dev/logos/`.

No account, device identifier, or user profile is sent with these requests.
As with any HTTPS request, models.dev sees your IP address and standard request headers when you download the catalog.
That data is handled under the models.dev privacy practices, not by ModeLLM.

Links in the app may open the models.dev website or the ModeLLM GitHub repository in your browser.

## Data stored on your device

ModeLLM keeps a local copy of the catalog in an on-device database so the app works offline.
It also stores your app preferences locally.

This data never leaves your device, except that Android may include app data in your device's encrypted backup if you have backups enabled.
Uninstalling ModeLLM deletes all of it.

## Third-party services

ModeLLM talks to no third-party services other than models.dev, described above.

## Children

ModeLLM is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.
The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this policy will be updated here with a new "Last updated" date.
